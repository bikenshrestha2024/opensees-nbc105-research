# ConcreteCM author command example

DIRECT PUBLISHED NUMERICAL EXAMPLE | Not M30 calibration. Shape parameters copied from author example; stresses converted ksi to MPa.

Chang-Mander/Tsai backbone: sigma=fpcc*n*x/[1+(n-r/(r-1))*x+x^r/(r-1)], x=eps/epcc, n=Ec*epcc/fpcc. Critical strain and tensile branches plus native cyclic rules use rc,xcrn,ft,et,rt,xcrp. Formula is not a replacement custom solver.

Source: https://opensees.berkeley.edu/wiki/index.php/ConcreteCM_-_Complete_Concrete_Model_by_Chang_and_Mander_%281994%29
