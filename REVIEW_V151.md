## Gravity versus seismic weight
Mass factors and gravity factors are separate configuration dictionaries. A value in mass_factors does not automatically authorize a gravity strength combination. NBC 105:2025 Table 5-1 uses lambda=0.30 for other occupancy, 0.60 for storage and zero roof live in seismic weight. Time-history clauses 8.1.1.2 and 9.3.1.2 explicitly refer gravity loading to 5.2; thus D+lambda L can also be the TH preload basis. This is distinct from gravity strength checks such as 1.2D+1.5L and the IS16700 initial PDelta combination. Keep the actual purpose, occupancy and roof treatment explicit. Changing ULS/SLS stiffness alone does not change the selected gravity profile. AS_CONFIGURED preserves user factors and does not certify their basis. No factors were silently changed.

## Grade cards and visual diagnostics
Visual material workbench now opens M30/M35/M40/M45 and Fe415/Fe500/Fe550 input cards. Measured parameters remain null when no defensible specimen card is available. Concrete cube grade is not cylinder fpc. Concrete01 peak strain/residual/ultimate strain and Steel02 hardening/isotropic parameters cannot be inferred from grade labels. Supplied cards generate native OpenSees curves in an isolated process, PNG, CSV, formula-based unit-conversion Excel chart and recorded-path animation/GIF export. This is response generation, not automatic experimental validation. R0=20,cR1=.925,cR2=.15 are documented transition choices, not experimental Fe-grade calibration.

Experimental source identified: Carreño, Lotfizadeh, Conte & Restrepo (2020), J Structural Engineering 146,04019205, https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)ST.1943-541X.0002505. SimCenter provides 36 UCSD cyclic coupons, Grade60 ASTM A615/A706, calibration workflow and data: https://nheri-simcenter.github.io/quoFEM-Documentation/common/user_manual/examples/desktop/qfem-0028/README.html . This release links the original dataset; it does not bundle/falsely relabel its fitted parameters as Indian Fe grades. Exact requested-grade experimental cards are still pending traceable data and calibration.

Concrete01 implementation: https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/material/uniaxialMaterials/Concrete01.html .
